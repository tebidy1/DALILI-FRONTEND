Institutional continuity

Your experts' know-how is among your most valuable assets. Make it stay.

Procedures are documented as they're performed: a screenshot for every step, with the expert's spoken explanation transcribed beneath it. No separate documentation sessions.

When an expert moves to another role or leaves the organization, their method remains a working reference for whoever follows — and through rotation and localization cycles, the incoming person inherits the complete method, not a summary of it.

Every guide carries the expert's name and voice — so their knowledge becomes an institutional record that bears its author's name, not a set of answers given a hundred times.

Arabic transcription accuracy of WER 8.3%, enforced by a release gate that blocks publishing if it slips.
