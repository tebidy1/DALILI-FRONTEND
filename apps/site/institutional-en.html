How it works

Your expert does the work once. ITQAN captures, publishes, and guides.

  1. 1

    Captures

    The expert works as usual; every step is saved with its screenshot and their spoken explanation written beneath it.

  2. 2

    Publishes

    The guide reaches the team as a link that opens with no account and no install — or from the organization's library.

  3. 3

    Guides

    Employees open it beside their work, or choose “Train me” and it walks them step by step over the live system.
