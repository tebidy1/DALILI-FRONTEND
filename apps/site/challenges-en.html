When the expert leaves, the method leaves with them.

“Ask them, they're the only one who knows.” Every time that sentence is said, part of your organization is living in one person's head.

What happens today

  1. 42%

    of institutional knowledge is held by one person and no one else. When they leave, it leaves.Panopto / YouGov, 2018

  2. 14.1%

    employee turnover in Saudi Arabia, the highest in the GCC — and 25–30% in retail.Procapita, 2024 · Quest Search, 2025

  3. 50–150%

    of annual salary to replace a departing employee, while the successor works at 40–60% lower output through the handover.Quest Search, 2025

With ITQAN

The method stays, even when its owner moves on.

  • The procedure is documented as it is performed. A screenshot for every step, with the expert's spoken explanation written beneath it. No documentation sessions, no writing project.

  • The successor inherits the whole method. Not a summary of it — through replacement and localization cycles as much as internal moves.

  • Expertise is credited to its owner. Every guide carries the expert's name and voice, so their knowledge stays a known contribution.

Arabic transcription accuracy of WER 8.3%, enforced by a release gate that blocks publishing if it slips.

Every procedure not yet documented belongs to your organization only while its owner is at their desk.

Somewhere in your branches, one employee completes the transaction faster and with fewer errors. Their method has never left their desk.

What happens today

  1. Systematic

    That describes the gap between the best performer and the average: individual performance follows a power law, not a normal distribution.O'Boyle & Aguinis · Personnel Psychology, 2012

  2. SAR 9,200

With ITQAN

Your best method becomes everyone's method.

  • One version for every branch. The procedure perfected at one site is published to all from the organization's library.

  • Updates reach everyone. When a guide is updated, it is updated for everyone who opens it. No old copies in circulation.

  • It arrives as a link. Opens with no account and no install, and can be sent to whoever needs it.

Every day the best method stays in one branch, the other branches pay the difference.

Quality certifications5 of 6

The procedure was written for the auditor, not for the person doing the work.

Before the audit, files are updated and signatures collected. After it, they go back into the folder, and work goes back to habit.

What happens today

  1. Documented

    is what the certificate proves — not that the procedure is performed as documented.

  2. 7.5

    the clause that asks for documented information available and suitable for use, where and when it is needed. The procedure sits in a shared folder, far from the working screen.ISO 9001:2015 · 7.5.3

  3. Silently

    is how a procedure goes stale when the system changes — and no one notices before the next audit.

With ITQAN

Beyond what the certificate requires: quality in every step of daily work.

  • What is performed is what was documented. The procedure opens over the working screen itself, and the employee follows it step by step.

  • Every change with its author and date. A version history shows what changed, when, and who changed it.

  • Whoever performs it reports it. When a step no longer matches the screen, their note reaches the procedure's owner from inside the guide.

  • A published standard. ITQAN's five-point standard is built on the requirements of clause 7.5, and we publish the status of each condition today. Read the standard

Audit readiness is not a guarantee of passing an audit or obtaining a certification.

A procedure that goes stale unseen is an audit finding waiting for its date.
